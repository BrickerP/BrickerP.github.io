# 01 — Brainstorm

## Hypothesis

Confidence: **90%**

The next identity gain will come from making the road itself carry the
LOOP signature, not from adding interface or more content.

## How might we

How might we make a still frame and five-second excerpt recognizable as
`LOOP 01` while preserving the authored first-person Beijing world?

## Not for

- A driving-game audience
- A productivity or AI-usage dashboard
- Visitors looking for navigation or geographic accuracy
- A generic neon/cyberpunk portfolio

## Directions considered

| Direction | Value | Feasibility | Difference | Hidden assumption |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Carrier Line / Open Circuit | Makes identity scene-native | High | One carrier and one closure node | The road remains readable in every viewport |
| Four Vermilion Thresholds | Strong cinematic rhythm | Medium | Four Beijing spatial gates | The connection to LOOP is self-evident |
| Exhibit Label | Fastest, safest reframe | Very high | Catalog-style chrome | Interface alone is memorable enough |

## Recommendation

**Carrier Line / Open Circuit.** It reuses the approved card signature, keeps
motion as the content, and needs no new product surface.
