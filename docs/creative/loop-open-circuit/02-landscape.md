# 02 — Landscape

## Positioning

**A quiet, continuous Beijing circuit whose single carrier line makes the city
feel inhabited—not a WebGL technology demo.**

## Accessibility calibration

The [W3C reduced-motion preference](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediaqueries-5/#prefers-reduced-motion)
supports minimizing non-essential motion. The LOOP fallback therefore remains
an intentionally composed still until explicit Play; it is not a degraded or
blank version of the experience.

## Interaction to copy

- Motion remains the content.
- Controls stay compact, literal, and secondary.
- One motif can bridge world, chrome, static poster, and recording.
- Mobile retains the complete experience without hover or drag.

## Traps to avoid

- Decorative HUD noise
- Generic automotive telemetry
- Multiple glowing landmarks
- Mobile as a cropped desktop afterthought
- Reduced motion that hides information or controls
